## Description (up to 4000 chars)

```
Tiderunner is a top-down buoy race down winding rivers, harbours and fjords. Push the throttle, mind the marks, ride the current, catch a boost — and if you spend too long aground, something in deep water will come looking.

SEVENTEEN COURSES
From the wide meanders of Ouse Bends and long fjord sweeps of Atlantic Leg, to the relentless helm-work of Corryvreckan and Hell's Mouth. Monaco Harbour and Staithes Twist give you hairpins between stone walls. The Broadwater fields a full twelve-boat fleet three abreast. Each course has its own bank width, corner count and personality.

FOUR SKILL TIERS
Easy, Normal, Hard and Insane. Up to Hard, the tiers are driving skill rather than horsepower — how much of the corner speed a rival will use, how much river it takes to straighten a bend, how steady it holds a line, how fast it moves the helm, and whether it checks for clear water before grabbing a boost. From Normal upward the rivals race each other too: tucking into the wake ahead for the tow, then pulling out to pass when there's room.

MOMENTUM-BASED HANDLING
The hull carries its speed through a turn, so ease off before the mark and let the stern come round. Astern is available but slow. Ride another boat's wake for a pull; grab yellow surges for a proper boost.

SHARKS ON THE FLATS
Stay aground too long and something in the deep water notices. It'll come up, run you down, and take the hull under. Keep to the channel and you're safe.

ONE-HANDED PLAY
Hold anywhere on screen to summon a floating analog joystick — push in the direction you want to go and the boat responds. No fixed buttons, no thumbs in the wrong place.

BUILT LIGHT
Offline. No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Play once and never look at a network again.

Records for best lap and best 2/3/5-lap total are kept per course in local storage on your device.
```
