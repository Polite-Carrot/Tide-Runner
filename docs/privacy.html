Tiderunner

Privacy · updated August 2026

Tiderunner collects no personal data.

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not contact any server — there is nothing to send.

Course records, best lap times, your chosen name, and audio preferences (music on/off, game sounds on/off) are stored in your browser or app's local storage on the device itself. They never leave the device. Clearing the app's storage, or deleting the app, removes everything.

Tiderunner does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, health data, motion sensors, HealthKit, HomeKit, or any other iOS or Android sensitive API.

If you have any questions, contact the developer via the project repository: github.com/Polite-Carrot/Tide-Runner.
