Tiderunner

Privacy · updated September 2026

Tiderunner collects no personal data.

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It does not otherwise contact any server — there is nothing else to send.

The web version uses Google Analytics (GA4) to see which courses and difficulties get played, so we know where to spend effort next. It records gameplay events — which course, what difficulty, lap count, and whether a race was finished or abandoned — and the usual page-level information GA4 collects automatically (roughly: your browser, device type, approximate region from your IP address, and how you arrived at the page). It does not see your chosen in-game name, and nothing you type anywhere in the game is sent to it. Google's own privacy policy covers what they do with it: policies.google.com/privacy.

Send usage data, in the game's own Settings, turns this off — no reload needed, and no more than the first request that happened before you had a chance to open Settings, same as any first visit to any website. It's on by default, and your choice is remembered the same way as your music and sound preferences.

The iOS and Android apps do not currently include Google Analytics or any other analytics — this section will be updated if and when that changes.

Course records, best lap times, your chosen name, and audio preferences (music on/off, game sounds on/off) are stored in your browser or app's local storage on the device itself. They never leave the device. Clearing the app's storage, or deleting the app, removes everything.

Tiderunner does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, health data, motion sensors, HealthKit, HomeKit, or any other iOS or Android sensitive API.

If you have any questions, contact the developer via the project repository: github.com/Polite-Carrot/Tide-Runner.
