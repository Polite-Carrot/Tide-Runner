Tiderunner

Privacy · updated September 2026

Tiderunner has no user accounts, and your progress never leaves your device.

Everything you earn — courses, skins, coins, gear and lap times — is stored on the device you played on and is never uploaded. The captain name you can set is stored the same way and shown only to you. The one way any of it leaves is the transfer code in Settings, and only when you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

Adverts. The iOS and Android apps show adverts: optional ones you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ones between races. They are served by Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and basic device information. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form appears before any personalised advert is served, and you can change that choice at any time under Privacy & data in Settings — refusing stops adverts being personalised, it does not remove them. Google's advertising notice covers what they do with it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The web version at this address shows no adverts at all.

The iOS and Android apps can use Firebase Analytics, but only if you say yes. Nothing is recorded until you accept, either on the prompt shown the first time you open the game or the Send usage data toggle in Settings — analytics collection is switched off by default in the app itself and is only turned on once you accept. If you accept, it records gameplay events — which course, what difficulty, lap count, and whether a race was finished or abandoned — along with the basic device and approximate-region information Firebase collects automatically. It does not see your chosen in-game name, and nothing you type anywhere in the game is sent to it. Google's own privacy policy covers what they do with it: policies.google.com/privacy.

Send usage data, in the game's own Settings, is the same choice — change your mind any time, no reload needed. Declining, or ignoring the banner entirely, costs you nothing: the game plays exactly the same either way. Your choice is remembered the same way as your music and sound preferences.

The web version at this address has no analytics at all. It loads no analytics script, makes no analytics request, and records nothing about how it is played — whatever you choose on the prompt. Google Analytics was removed from it entirely; the Send usage data choice only has an effect inside the iOS and Android apps.

Course records, best lap times, your chosen name, and audio preferences (music on/off, game sounds on/off) are stored in your browser or app's local storage on the device itself. They never leave the device. Clearing the app's storage, or deleting the app, removes everything.

Tiderunner does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, health data, motion sensors, HealthKit, HomeKit, or any other iOS or Android sensitive API.

If you have any questions, contact the developer via the project repository: github.com/Polite-Carrot/Tide-Runner.
